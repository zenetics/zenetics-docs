# How to Get Started with ZENETICS

Welcome to ZENETICS! We're excited to have you here. This guide will help you get started with ZENETICS and show you how to create your first project. The process is simple and will get you up and running with a first sample application in less then 10 minutes.

This quickstart guide will walk you through the necessary steps to evaluate the first output generated by your application. This guide assumes that you have an existing ZENETICS account.

If you don't have an account yet, you can request a ZENETICS Product Demo or join our Waiting List for the Pilot Program. To request a demo, book your personal demo with our team at [zenetics.io](https://www.zenetics.io/talk-to-sales). To join our **Free Trial**, please reach out to us at: sales@zenetics.io

<Steps>

### Step 1: Create Your First Test Suite in ZENETICS

Your first steps is to setup your account in ZENETICS and create a first Test Suite that you can run on your local development environment.

Head over to the **[🔖 ZENETICS Test Management Quickstart](/test-management/quickstart)** to get started with your first Test Suite.

If you don't have an account yet, reach out to our team to help you get started with your Free Trial: [ZENETICS Sales Team](https://www.zenetics.io/talk-to-sales)

### STEP 2: Setup the ZENETICS SDK to Run Your Test Suite

The ZENETICS SDK for Python provides an easy way to run your test suites managed in ZENETICS on your local development environment or on your continuous integration (CI/CD) system. The SDK comes with command line interface (CLI) tools to enable you to run test suites with a simple command.

Head over to the **[🔖 ZENETICS SDK Quickstart](/sdk/quickstart)** to get started with the ZENETICS SDK and learn how to run your first test suite on your local development environment.

### 🎉 You Are All Set

Congratulations! You have successfully created your first Test Suite in ZENETICS and setup the ZENETICS SDK to run your test suites on your local development environment. You are now ready to start building and optimizing reliable LLM applications with ZENETICS.

</Steps>
